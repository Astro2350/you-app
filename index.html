How do I delete my account?

Go to You › Settings › Network account › Delete network account. This removes your account and everything tied to it from our server straight away. Settings › Data › Delete all data also removes everything on your iPhone.

How do I report or block someone?

Press and hold anyone in a league, an index fund, a quote or your watchlist, then choose Report or Block. You'll find the people you've blocked in Settings › Blocked.

Is the money real?

No. The Floor is a game played with play money. It can't be bought, cashed out or exchanged, and nothing in $YOU is investment advice.

Does my Apple Health data leave my phone?

No. Health data is read on your iPhone and used only for your own report. If you share a headline, it's written without it. See the privacy policy.
