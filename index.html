How do I delete my account?

Go to You › Settings › Account › Delete account. This removes your account and everything tied to it from our server straight away. Settings › Data › Delete all data also removes everything on your iPhone.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap … next to anyone in a league, an index fund, a portfolio or your watchlist (or press and hold them), then choose Report or Block. You'll find the people you've blocked in Settings › Blocked.

Is the money real?

No. $STOCKS is a game played with play money. You start with $10,000, checking in and ticking habits pay you more, and if you run low you can start over with a fresh $10,000. Play money can't be cashed out or exchanged, and nothing in $YOU is investment advice.

Does my Apple Health data leave my phone?

Not the measurements, ever. Health data is read on your iPhone for your own report, and a headline you share is written without it. If you choose to join the $STOCKS area boards, your daily area scores can reflect it (never your heart rate), and turning that off deletes them. See the privacy policy.
