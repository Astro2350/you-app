Privacy policy

Effective 21 September 2026 · updated for sector boards

$YOU is a daily journal that turns your day into a stock price. It works without an account, and most of what you put into it never leaves your iPhone. This policy explains what stays on your device, what reaches our server if you choose to join the network, and how to delete it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your journal: check-ins, notes, best moments and drains, ratings, sector moves, habits, guidance, rally sessions, reports, your price and your history.
  • Photos are stored on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Camera: rally sessions are analysed live on the device and never recorded.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: dictation is turned into text on the device.
  • Apple Health (optional and read-only): sleep, steps, workouts and resting heart rate. These are used to score and write your own daily report on the device. The measurements are never uploaded, never shared and never used for advertising, and a headline you share is written without them. The one exception is optional and yours to choose: see sector scores below.
  • AI: reports and photo readings are written on the device, either by Apple Intelligence (Apple's on-device Foundation Models) or by the app's built-in engine. Nothing you write, photograph or record is sent to a cloud AI service.
  • Widgets read a small summary (your price and streak) saved in the app's own storage on the device.

The network (optional)

Nothing is sent to our server unless you tap Connect and agree to the community rules. Connecting creates an anonymous account, with no email, phone number or password. After that we store:

  • a random account ID;
  • your ticker, your display name (if you set one) and your sharing choices;
  • days you choose to publish. Each day is published only when you press Publish on it, and includes the date, the day's move (if you share movement), the headline and summary (if you share the headline, written without Health data), and market events such as a five-day rally. Your notes are never uploaded;
  • who you add to your watchlist, and your reactions to their days;
  • the Floor, if you list there: your public price's daily move and its audit score. Both are worked out on your iPhone. They can reflect activity that the camera or Apple Health verified, but none of that underlying data is sent. We also store your play-money wallet, holdings, trades, league standings, and the index funds you create or join;
  • sector scores, only if you join the Floor's sector boards (off unless you turn it on). With each posted day we store one score per sector, such as Fitness +0.80. Scores are worked out on your iPhone from your audited day, so they can reflect steps, workouts and sleep read from Apple Health; they never include the measurements themselves or your heart rate, and Health readings that count against a day are left out. Other people on the Floor see your weekly totals on the boards, and from them could work out roughly how you slept or moved. Turning sector scores off in Settings deletes every score you've shared, straight away;
  • the people you block, and the reports you make (the reason, where you saw them and any note you add).

We use this only to run these features. We don't sell it, use it for advertising or track you across apps and websites, and the app contains no analytics or advertising software.

Who sees it: people who add your ticker (only if you've made it findable) see what you publish. People on the Floor see your ticker, display name, public price and league position. People you block can't see you, and you can't see them.

Service providers

The network is run on Supabase (database and sign-in), hosted in the United States (Ohio). Apple provides the on-device AI and Health frameworks, which don't send your data to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Server data is kept until you delete it.
  • Settings › Account › Delete network account removes your account and everything tied to it from our server straight away. That includes your profile, published days, watchlist, reactions, Floor listing, wallet, holdings, trades, index memberships and blocks.
  • Reports you made about others are kept without your identity, so moderation can finish.
  • Index funds you founded stay for their other members, without you.
  • Settings › Data › Delete all data does all of the above and also deletes everything on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes everything on the device.

You can also email us to ask for a copy or deletion of your data.

Children

$YOU isn't directed at children under 13, and the network is for people aged 13 and over.

Security

All traffic to our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules mean each account can read and change only what it's allowed to.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change affects what leaves your device, the app will tell you first.
